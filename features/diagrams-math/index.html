    Math (LaTeX)¶

    Inline math: \(E = mc^2\) and \(a^2 + b^2 = c^2\).

    Display math:

    \[ \int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2} \]

    A matrix:

    \[ A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \det A = ad - bc \]

    Aligned equations:

    \[ \begin{aligned} (a+b)^2 &= a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \\ (a-b)^2 &= a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \end{aligned} \]

    Greek letters and sums: \(\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}\)
